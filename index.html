01

Research

Accepted at ICICT 2027

Tarsus: Latency-Optimal Policy-Decoupled Distributed Storage

Nick Tagliamonte, Justin Y. Shi, Garrett Stoll

Tarsus is a distributed storage system that separates replication policy from the data path. I designed and implemented the system and developed its experimental evaluation. The paper has been accepted and will be published at ICICT 2027.

The related storage architecture is also the subject of a pending patent, Probabilistic Decentralized Storage via Provider-Data Decoupling, on which I’m a co-inventor.
